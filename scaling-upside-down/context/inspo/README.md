# Reference board: the "aura" home page (19 Nov 2026)

James asked (19 Nov) for a home page with "more aura", less symbolism, text set in interesting ways and no
left-aligned column, drawn from Awwwards and not from the head. This is the board it was built from.

How it was gathered: awwwards.com itself refuses connections from the cloud session (connection reset / 502),
so its lists were read through a fetch tool: Sites of the Day (page 1, to 18 Nov 2026), the Typography winners
(page 1), and the two book sites that won Site of the Day (Aardvark Book Club, Obys' Design Books). The real sites
were then opened in a browser at 1440×900 and screenshot at three scroll depths. James's taste repo
(`joe51111jwd/james-taste`) could not be attached to the session (permission denied), so its SKILL.md and
checklists were not used; this board is the only taste input.

Files: `2026-11-19-<site>-<n>.jpg` (1 = first screen, 2-3 = scrolled). `2026-11-19-before-live-site.jpg` is the
live home page before this change (12 screens through the scroll).

## The direction

**The book in a dark room, lit.** A warm near-black ground with grain, the real hardcover glowing in the middle of
the first screen, and Michael's words set large around it. The page reads as a sequence of screens, one idea
each: the opening (the book and its subtitle); the problem (the tower of ALWAYS planks that falls, the ledger, the
pile, "That's scaling upside down." turning over); the idea (Up, Side and Down as three coloured bands that stack
into the cover); the book; the one sign-up.

What it drops: the block story a visitor had to decode. (Mid-build James said this one need not keep the blocks at
all; it is an alternative to the blocks home page, not its replacement.) The blocks stay as the book's identity (the cover, the
colours, two real renders used as pictures that illustrate a sentence), not as the language of the page.
