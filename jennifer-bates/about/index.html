About

Jennifer G. Bates

Jennifer G. Bates is the founder and CEO of HumanFactor™ and the originator of Stakeholder Risk, the formal category of enterprise risk that comes from the relationships, dynamics, incentives, governance and decision systems among the stakeholders who determine a company’s outcome.

A six-time founder and 23-year entrepreneur based in New York, Jennifer has advised CEOs, boards, investors and fiduciary decision-makers across life sciences, technology, finance and private equity. She created the proprietary frameworks behind Stakeholder Diligence™, the HumanFactor Index™ and RealTime Advisory™.

Her work spans more than 25 industries and has impacted more than $4.2 billion in enterprise value, including valuation increases of up to 800% and multiple seven- and eight-figure exits.

She advises biotech and life sciences founders, CEOs, boards and investors, and is a frequent keynote speaker. She holds an MS in Finance, NYU Stern School of Business.
