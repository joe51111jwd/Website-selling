Speaking

The human side of enterprise value, on stage.

Jennifer G. Bates is a frequent keynote speaker for investors, boards, founders and life sciences leaders. Every talk brings the audience the same thing: a way to see the risk in the room that no model prices.

Request Jennifer

Keynotes

Four talks.

  1. 01

    Why deals fail

    The stakeholder risk inside the model

    Most deals that miss do not miss on the spreadsheet. They miss on the people the spreadsheet assumed would agree. How to see that before close, and price it.

    For private equity, M&A and investor audiences

  2. 02

    Keep your authority, protect your number

    For founders through a capital transition

    A raise, a new board seat or a sale changes who decides. What founders lose when they don’t plan for that, and how the strongest ones keep control of the outcome.

    For founders, CEOs, executive teams

  3. 03

    Pricing the human system

    Stakeholder Risk as a measurable category

    Markets learned to put a number on volatility, credit and governance. Why the human system is next, and what boards can measure today.

    For boards, lenders, fiduciaries, risk leaders

  4. 04

    From science to enterprise value

    Stakeholder Risk in life sciences

    In life sciences the science is diligenced to the decimal and the team is taken on trust. Where that goes wrong between the lab and the exit.

    For biotech founders, boards and investors
