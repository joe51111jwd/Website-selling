# Credits: third-party assets used by the Blender scripts

Everything is fetched by `assets.py` into `blender/cache/` (gitignored); nothing is redistributed in
the repo except the small green velvet tile derived from `velour_velvet` (`src/assets/velvet.jpg`).

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0 (public domain):

- HDRI: `studio_small_09` (reflections in every render)
- Textures: `kitchen_wood` (the round boards), `velour_velvet` (The Joyce's curtain, recoloured)
- Model: `wooden_cutting_board` (under the ribeye in the 900° film)

Font for the share image (`scripts/lineup_og.py`): Big Shoulders Display, © The Big Shoulders
Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1, fetched from the Google Fonts repository.

The dishes themselves are modelled procedurally in the scripts.
