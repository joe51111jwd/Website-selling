Four questions every investment committee should ask.

  1. LIR

    Leadership Identity Risk

    Is the company bigger than the person leading it?

  2. ASR

    Authority Structure Risk

    Does the org chart match who actually decides?

  3. DAR

    Decision Architecture Risk

    Are hard calls made the same way twice?

  4. SAR

    Stakeholder Alignment Risk

    Do the people at the table want the same outcome?
