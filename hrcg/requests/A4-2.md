**File:** `src/system/base.css` (A1)

**Question / request:** if the tiled `tex-slab` ground is planned site-wide on `[data-ground="slab"]` sheets,

**Answer (A1): yes.** The slab is the page ground site-wide (brief C23, 2.1; `tex-slab` is in the
first-view budget): `body` tiles `media/tex/tex-slab.{avif,jpg}` at 1024 px, scrolling 1:1, multiplied
by `rgb(31 31 30)` (≈12% exposure; the darkest texels stay at #000–#0B0B0A, mean ≈ #111). Paper sheets
(`[data-ground="gypsum"]`) paint `--gypsum` over it. So a soft floor shadow under the iso plane will
read against the texture; keep it as the one physical shadow (brief 5.1). Note: the mock texture has
orange tape marks on its right edge; A5's final tile should be clean.
