Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to provide a smooth user experience and to collect basic analytics information. We do not have a separate cookie policy page, so we describe our cookie practices here.

What Are Cookies? Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your browser and remember certain information (such as user preferences) over time. Cookies can be "session" cookies (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

How We Use Cookies: SmartCrew HQ uses cookies for purposes such as:

  • Essential Functions: Some cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. For example, they may be used to remember your input on a form as you navigate between pages, or to keep you logged in (if our site offers account login features). These cookies enable core functionality and without them the site may not operate correctly.
  • Preferences and Convenience: We may use cookies to remember any preferences you set on our site to enhance your experience. For instance, if our site allows you to choose a language or toggle certain settings, a cookie might save those preferences so you don’t have to set them every time you visit.
  • Analytics: We might use our own first-party analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site (e.g., which pages are visited most, how long people stay on a page). This information is collected in an aggregated and anonymous form – it does not identify you personally. The insights we gain from analytics cookies help us improve website performance and content. (Note: We do not use Google Analytics or any similar third-party analytics services, so your data is not being sent to outside analytics providers.)

No Third-Party Cookies: We do not use third-party cookies or trackers on our site. This means we do not embed cookies for advertising networks, social media platforms, or external data analytics companies. All cookies that our site sets are first-party (coming directly from SmartCrew HQ) and are used solely to support the functionality described above. Because of this, when you visit our site, you should not see cookies used for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

Your Choices Regarding Cookies: You have the ability to control or limit how cookies are used on your browser. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being placed on your device. Please be aware that if you disable cookies entirely, some parts of our website may not work properly. For example, essential functions (like form submissions or preference settings) might be affected. However, you will still be able to view most content on our site even with all cookies disabled.

If you do not want to receive cookies, you can also delete cookies that have already been set through your browser settings. For more detailed information on managing cookies, you can check your browser’s help documentation. (Note: Our site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals, but since we do not engage in cross-site tracking or advertising, your browsing on SmartCrew HQ is not profiled for advertising purposes.)
