Hang It Upside Down

Homepage idea ranked 10 of 15 · storyboard: what you see as you scroll

1. First screen: strings hang from a board in arches

Every hire compounds complexity before it compounds capacity.

2. Scrolling: each hire hangs one more weight (red) and every curve shifts. At 15 it tangles

Past twelve, something breaks.

3. The ask: the camera turns into the mirror. The net, flipped, stands as a vault

You can't build a building with only height. You need all three dimensions or it topples.
