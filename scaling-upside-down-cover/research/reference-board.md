## What the trend writing says

From [SPINE, "Book cover design trends in 2025 and predictions for 2026"](https://spinemagazine.co/articles/book-cover-design-trends-in-2025-predictions-for-2026) (fiction-led, but the points carry over):
- **"One-colour covers"**: purposeful type with one or two motifs on a flat field, text at the top and/or bottom,
  and large empty areas. That is the frame of all three directions.
- **Anti-AI covers (2026)**: visible grain and texture, and "imperfect yet purposeful lettering". SPINE expects AI
  covers to look "flat", with type "placed on top". Michael's mockup is exactly that, which is why James wants it
  replaced. Answer: real objects (the Blender stack, real wood grain, stamp ink that skips on the grain) and type
  that is part of the picture, not dropped on it.
- **The serialized standalone**: a consistent imprint look. The cover, the site and later the book's diagrams
  should share one kit: three paints, Anton, Playfair Black stamps and a Caslon text face. Michael asked for "a
  consistent visual identity for the book".

## Thumbnail rules (Amazon shows the cover about 150 px wide)

From the search on thumbnail legibility ([The Book Designer](https://www.thebookdesigner.com/15-ebook-covers-success-and-failure-in-the-kindle-store/),
[Written Word Media](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/the-5-most-common-mistakes-in-book-cover-design-and-how-to-avoid-them/),
[Creative Paramita](https://creativeparamita.com/book-cover/book-cover-thumbnail-that-survives-the-amazon-test/)):
thumbnails are about 100-160 px; thin and script type disappears; the title must read; drop shadows, bevels and
fine detail vanish; contrast must hold. These are vendor blogs, not Amazon's own guidance. Every direction is
checked at 150 px and 100 px (`out/thumbs/`).

## What this means for the three directions

- **A, The Stack** (close to the mockup): keep Michael's identity and make it exact. A real render; SCALING the
  height of the stack; one stamp size; one grid; the subtitle and author in a quiet book face.
- **B, Upside-Down** (bold, type only): the title does what it says. UP sits upright, SIDE lies on its side and
  DOWN hangs upside down, on full-bleed bands of the three paints on a black ground: a poster you can read across a
  room.
- **C, The Cycle** (conceptual): the book's model as a chart. Up, sideways, a small step down, repeat: the line
  climbs past the dashed "scaling trap", which spikes and crashes. The title words are the chart's labels.
